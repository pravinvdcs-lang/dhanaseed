Guide 01

Navigating RBI's LRS Limits

Under the Reserve Bank of India’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), resident individuals can freely remit up to $250,000 USD per financial year for permitted capital and current account transactions. The intended DhanaSeed experience may incorporate TCS information, A2-form workflows and FX visibility; actual features and regulatory treatment will depend on the final product and applicable rules.

Guide 02

India Access from Anywhere

Global Indians face complex account setups across NRE, NRO, and PIS accounts. The intended DhanaSeed experience is designed to organize repatriation information, foreign exchange considerations and tax-related workflows for eligible NRIs and OCIs; actual availability will depend on the final product, partners and applicable rules.

Guide 03

Multi-Currency Risk & Tax Intelligence

Cross-border investing introduces foreign exchange volatility and dual-tax considerations. The intended intelligence layer may present home-currency return context, foreign-tax information and multi-jurisdiction transaction records; tax treatment should be confirmed with qualified advisers.
