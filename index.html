Guide 01

Navigating RBI's LRS Limits

Under the Reserve Bank of India’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), resident individuals can freely remit up to $250,000 USD per financial year for permitted capital and current account transactions. DhanaSeed simplifies compliance by tracking TCS threshold calculations, facilitating seamless A2 form workflows, and maintaining real-time FX transparency.

Guide 02

India Access from Anywhere

Global Indians face complex account setups across NRE, NRO, and PIS accounts. DhanaSeed consolidates repatriation reporting, foreign exchange handling, and cross-border tax compliance, empowering NRIs and OCIs to allocate seamlessly back into Indian equities and debt instruments.

Guide 03

Multi-Currency Risk & Tax Intelligence

Cross-border investing introduces foreign exchange volatility and dual-tax considerations. DhanaSeed calculates true real returns in your primary currency, accounts for foreign tax credits (FTC), and unifies multi-jurisdiction capital gain ledgers.
